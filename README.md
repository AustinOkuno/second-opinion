# second-opinion

### Is this message a scam? Get a second opinion.

---

## The Problem

Scams and phishing are a growing problem in America. Every day, scam messages are sent out and aimed at people who may not be familiar with the dangers of scams and phishing attacks. Many people simply don't have the awareness to tell what's real and what's fake.

## Our Solution

**Second Opinion** is an app that gives you perspective and answers one simple question:

> **Is this message a scam?**

Our website is built to be elderly friendly, so anyone can use it. Just drop in your message, and our trusty red panda, **Scampy**, will give you an answer!

## How It Works

1. **Drop your message.** Paste the text, or upload a screenshot.
2. **Let Scampy take a look.** Scampy checks the message for you.
3. **Get your answer.** Find out whether it's a scam or the real thing.

## Features

- 📝 **Text or screenshot:** check a message either way
- 👵 **Elderly friendly design:** simple and easy for anyone to use
- 📖 **How-to-use guide:** step-by-step help for first-time users
- ♿ **Accessibility:** built so more people can use it
- 🐼 **Scampy the red panda:** your friendly guide to spotting the difference between scam and real
